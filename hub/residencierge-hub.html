0.0 · What Claude Code actually is

⏱ 4 min readPrerequisites: none

The name is the worst thing about it. Claude Code is not a coding tool with a coding name. It is a general assistant that lives on your computer with its sleeves rolled up.

The one idea that matters

The Claude you may have used in a browser can only talk. Claude Code can act: it reads your files, writes your documents, digs through your data, opens websites, and builds things, all on your own machine, all by conversation. When commands need running, it runs them. You make the calls.

What that looks like in practice

You sayIt does
"Summarise every file in this folder"Reads them all in seconds and gives you the three things that matter
"Turn that into a one-page brief"Creates a real document on your laptop, formatted and dated
"What does this flyer say?"Reads images too: flyers, whiteboard photos, screenshots, scans
"What do competitors charge?"Pulls live answers from the web into your working file

What you need

  1. A Claude account on a paid plan

    Pro or Max. Your Residency pre-work covered this; if in doubt, ask Jodee.

  2. Your own laptop, Mac or Windows

    With administrator rights, so you can install one app. Company-managed laptops sometimes block installs, which is why we ask in advance.

  3. Zero technical experience

    Truly none. If you can send a message, you can do this course.

Mindset for the week

Justin teaches the thinking in the room. These lessons are where you put your hands on it: you say it, you see it happen, you keep the result. By the time you point this at your real business, the loop already feels normal.

0.1 · Installation

⏱ 5 minutesPrerequisites: 0.0Done at the 8:30 setup clinic

One app to install: Claude Desktop. It contains everything, including the Code tab where the whole course happens.

Install Claude Desktop

  1. Download the app

    Go to claude.ai/download and grab the version for your machine, Mac or Windows.

  2. Install and open it

    Mac: drag it into Applications. Windows: run the installer and accept the prompts. If your laptop asks for an administrator password, that is normal.

  3. Sign in

    Use the same Claude account as your pre-work. The app opens looking like the Claude you know.

  4. Find the Code tab

    Top left of the app window. Click it. That is where the Residencierge lives, and where every lesson happens.

Good to know, as at August 2026

Inside the Code tab, the left-hand sidebar shows your chats and projects, not your files. Your files appear in the Files panel, opened from near the top right, right beside the conversation. The course shows you exactly where to click.

Troubleshooting

The install is blocked or asks for an admin password I don't have
Your laptop is company-managed. Raise your hand, this is the number one thing the setup clinic exists for.
I can't see a Code tab
Make sure you're in the desktop app, not the browser, and signed in to your paid account. Still missing? Raise your hand.
It's asking me about model or permission settings
Leave everything on its defaults. The course handles the rest.

0.2 · Get the course & start

⏱ 5 minutesPrerequisites: 0.1

One pasted prompt fetches the whole course onto your laptop and wakes up the concierge.

The magic paste

  1. Open the Code tab and start a new session

    Claude Desktop app, Code tab, new chat. When it asks which folder to work in, choose your home folder, the one with your name on it (not Documents, which lives in iCloud on a Mac).

  2. Set the model

    In the bar under the message box, click the model name and choose Sonnet 5 with thinking on Medium. You'll know exactly what this means soon enough.

  3. Paste this prompt

    Exactly as written. Claude fetches the course folder and follows the instructions inside it.

Paste into Claude CodeClone https://github.com/jodee-tff/residencierge into a new folder called residencierge, then read the file BOOTSTRAP.md inside it and follow its instructions exactly.

Claude will download the course folder, quietly check your setup, and then greet you by name. From there, just respond. The concierge waits for you at every step.

Starting each lesson

Lesson 1.1 begins automatically after the paste, and from there the course flows: each lesson ends by handing you the next command to type right where you are, like /start-1-3. The concierge tells you the two moments a fresh chat is needed, and walks you through both.

Taking a break?

Any time you come back: new chat pointed at the residencierge folder, then the command for the lesson you're up to. Nothing is ever lost, your work lives in the folder.

Troubleshooting

My Mac asked to install "command line developer tools"
Click Install and let it finish, then tell Claude to carry on. One-time thing.
Typing / doesn't show the start commands
Your chat probably isn't pointed at the course folder. Start a new chat with the residencierge folder as the project. Or paste: Read lessons/SCRIPT_INSTRUCTIONS.md then the lesson script and begin teaching immediately.
Something else entirely
Raise your hand. Jodee or Erik will sort it in under a minute.

1.2 · The inheritance

⏱ 15 minutesPrerequisites: 1.1
Run this lesson/start-1-2

Lessons flow on in the same chat. Returning after a break? New chat pointed at the residencierge folder, then the command.

Deano left you a folder. This lesson teaches you to actually see it: what the folders hold, what an .md file is, and how to watch files appear as you and the concierge make them.

What an .md file is

Almost every file in this course ends in .md, short for Markdown. It is just text with a few simple symbols for structure, and it is the format this whole world runs on. Humans read it, Claude reads it perfectly, and it will still open in fifty years. Think of it as a Word document with the forty layers of bloat removed.

You typeIt means
# Big headingA title
## Smaller headingA section
- itemA bullet point
**bold**bold

That table is most of Markdown. You never need to write it by hand, the concierge does, but now nothing in these files will look mysterious.

Seeing your files for real

In the Claude Desktop app (as at August 2026), the left-hand sidebar is navigation for chats and projects, not your files. Your files show in the Files panel, opened from near the top right of the Code tab, listing the whole project right beside the conversation. From then on you watch files appear there, in real time, as you create them by talking. (Prefer a real window? Finder or File Explorer beside the app works too, and the concierge can open it for you.)

The word of the week

A word of the week sits at the top of the handover notes, and the lesson asks you to open the file yourself and read it out. A small thing with a serious job: it proves you are looking at the real file with your own eyes, not taking the AI's word for anything. Verification is a habit worth keeping all week.

What you'll have by the end

  1. The Files panel, open

    Your whole project listed beside the conversation, live.

  2. Deano's notes, read both ways

    Once by you, once summarised by the concierge in seconds. The division of labour: it reads fast, you read smart.

  3. Your first created file

    An owner's log carrying the cafe name you chose and your own day-one theory about the loyalty problem, landing in a folder you saw empty a minute earlier.

Troubleshooting

Double-clicking the .md file opened some app I don't recognise
Fine. Any text app works, it's just text. Read it, find the word, close it.
I can't find the folder the concierge is talking about
Ask it: "open the folder for me". It will. Still lost? Raise your hand.

1.3 · Working the files

⏱ 20 minutesPrerequisites: 1.2
Run this lesson/start-1-3

Lessons flow on in the same chat. Returning after a break? New chat pointed at the residencierge folder, then the command.

The payoff lesson. You point the concierge at exact files, pull patterns out of the mess, read an image nobody opened, check the outside world, and solve what is quietly killing Deano's loyalty program.

The @ mention

Type @ in the message box and a file picker pops up. Choose a file and your message points at exactly that file, no guessing, no "that document about the thing". If the picker ever comes up empty on your machine, typing the file path in plain text works identically, the @ is a shortcut, nothing more.

The mystery, in three moves

  1. Patterns from one messy file

    You @ the customer reviews file and ask what patterns it holds. Customers love the coffee, but a cluster say their loyalty stamps "disappear". One question turns noise into a ranked list.

  2. A contradiction across files

    The concierge reads Deano's loyalty numbers and his notes together: about 500 cards handed out, stamps given constantly, 9 free coffees ever redeemed. Two files, one contradiction, and Deano had every clue for months without connecting them.

  3. The unopened picture

    The answer sits in attachments/, in the loyalty flyer nobody looked at. Claude reads images too, and it finds the villain in the small print. No spoilers here: open it in the lesson.

Then the outside world, then the brief

A quick piece of live web research shows how loyalty programs normally work, so your decision has an outside benchmark. Then you make the call, as the owner, and the concierge builds it into a board-ready one-pager: situation, evidence, benchmark, your recommendation, dated and filed in outputs/. A document that did not exist three minutes earlier, built from a mess, a picture and the live internet.

Why this lesson matters

This exact loop, point me at chaos, I find the pattern, you make the call, we file the brief, is what you will run on your own business for the rest of the Residency. The coffee shop is just the training wheels.

Troubleshooting

The @ picker shows nothing
Type the file path as plain text instead, it works the same. The concierge will already know if your machine has a custom setup, and the crew can tidy it later.
The web research step failed
Networks happen. The concierge says so plainly and carries on with known basics, clearly labelled. The lesson still lands.

1.4 · Commands & navigation

⏱ 12 minutesPrerequisites: 1.3The driving skills
Run this lesson/start-1-4

Lessons flow on in the same chat. Returning after a break? New chat pointed at the residencierge folder, then the command.

The driving skills: four moves that make every session faster, safer and cheaper to run, each practised the moment you meet it.

The four skills

  1. Slash commands are saved instructions

    Every /start you've typed is one: an instruction card living in the folder. Type / on its own and the menu appears. Businesses build decks of these.

  2. The screenshot move

    Anything on any screen confusing you? Screenshot it, paste it into the chat, ask. You'll do it once in the lesson and use it all week.

  3. Plan before do

    For anything bigger than a question, ask for a plan first. The concierge proposes, you approve, only then does anything happen. You'll use it to organise the inherited files, and watch the folder tidy itself on approval.

  4. Match the model to the job

    Run the same prompt on a small model and a big one, straight from the bar below the message box, and feel the difference yourself. Quick jobs on quick engines, deep work on deep ones.

Why this lesson matters

Plan-before-do is the backbone of Module 2. Nothing gets built there without a plan you've signed off first.

The whole app gets designed in plain English before anything is built. Changing a plan costs nothing; changing a built thing costs patience.

Software is rules written down

The cafe already runs on them: buy a coffee, get a stamp. Hit ten, the next one's free, card resets. That's the app; everything else is paint. The concierge plays your decisions back as plain when-then lines, and you read them like a contract.

The design table

  1. Needs, then wants

    The can't-live-without rules come locked in. The fun extras (confetti at ten, a head-start stamp, a cheeky promise line) are yours to pick from a menu.

  2. Assumptions on the table

    The concierge fills every gap with a sensible assumption and declares each one, so nothing hides. Correct any, or wave them through.

  3. Choose the look with your eyes

    Three ready-made directions open in your browser: Burleigh Sunrise, Midnight Gold, The Point Pop. Genuinely different worlds. Pick one, or mix.

  4. Sign the contract

    BUILD-PLAN.md captures rules, look and scope on one page. Lesson 2.3 builds exactly this.

2.3 · Build & iterate

⏱ 15 minutesPrerequisites: 2.2
Run this lesson/start-2-3

Lessons flow on in the same chat. Returning after a break? New chat pointed at the residencierge folder, then the command.

The plan becomes a working loyalty card, in your browser, in this lesson. Then you shape it by describing what you see.

The build

The concierge reads your signed plan and builds it. Then you test it properly: stamp it, close the page, open it again and watch the stamps still standing, the exact thing paper never managed. Stamp to ten and collect.

The iteration masterclass

The skill that matters more than the build. You describe what you see ("the stamps are too small"), name the spot if you can, ask for options ("show me two other button styles"), dictate exact words, undo anything ("change it back"), or go nuclear ("bin it, start again"). All free. And since the concierge reads images, screenshot the card and paste it in to point at things. This loop is precisely how you'll build your real app in the next 48 hours.

Troubleshooting

I asked for a change and the page looks the same
Refresh the browser tab. Still stale? Hold Shift while refreshing. Still? Raise your hand.
I want something that wasn't in the plan
Say so. The plan gets built first, then your new idea becomes the next iteration. That's the method working, not failing.

Workbench, vault, shopfront

Your laptop is the workbench, where changes happen. GitHub is the vault: every version of your project, kept forever, revertible any time, shareable with a team. Vercel (next lesson) is the shopfront the world sees. Change on the workbench, save to the vault, the shopfront updates.

The handshake

The concierge sets up the connector and starts the login. Your browser opens GitHub's page with a short code, you check it matches, you click approve. Your hands, your account, and no password ever goes anywhere near the chat. Then the card goes into a private vault on your account, version one, history begins.

Troubleshooting

I don't have a GitHub account
Create the free one at github.com yourself, your email and your password, then carry on. The concierge waits.
The code page stalled or timed out
Ask for a fresh code, second try. Still stuck? Raise your hand.
