# Burleigh Heads Coffee Co.

The little cafe on the point. Established 2007 by Dean "Deano" Maloney. New ownership as of this week.

## The Basics

- Location: Goodwin Terrace, Burleigh Heads, QLD, a two minute walk from the surf
- Seats: 28 inside, 16 on the deck
- Staff: 2 full time (Priya, head barista, and Callum), 5 casuals across the week
- Trading: 5:30am to 2pm, seven days
- Known for: the single origin rotation, macadamia cookies baked in-house, and Deano yelling "morning legend" at every regular

(Staff word of the month, per Priya's whiteboard: MACADAMIA. Don't ask, it's a staff thing.)

## What the Locals Say

The cafe has a fiercely loyal breakfast crowd of tradies, surfers and the school-run set. Weekends bring the Gold Coast brunch tourists and a queue out the door between 8 and 10am.

## The Loyalty Program

Launched March this year with a printed flyer and paper stamp cards at the till. Buy 9 coffees, the 10th is free. Deano ran it himself and the details are, in his words, "all sorted, mate".

## House Style Notes

- Everything is casual, warm and a bit cheeky
- We never bag out competitors, Burleigh runs on good vibes
- The cookie recipe is Priya's nan's and does not leave the building
